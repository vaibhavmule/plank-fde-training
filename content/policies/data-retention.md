## Scope

This policy covers data stored in WorkBot systems, including requests,
attachments, analytics exports and backup copies.

## Retention periods

- **Customer requests and approvals:** 3 years after the request closes.
- **Support conversations:** 2 years after the last reply.
- **Application logs:** 90 days, except security logs, which are kept 1 year.
- **Backups:** 35 days rolling.
- **Analytics exports:** 13 months.

## Deletion

When a retention period ends, the data is deleted on the next scheduled
cleanup. Backups expire on their own schedule and are not edited in place.

## Exceptions

Legal holds suspend deletion for the affected records. The legal team records
each hold and notifies the operations team before the next cleanup run.

Owners must be able to explain why any dataset is kept longer than the
periods above. Reach the operations team for help with deletion requests.

## Purpose

This policy sets the baseline for how everyone at the company uses company
systems, devices and data. It applies to employees, contractors and anyone
with access to a company account.

## Expected behaviour

- Use company accounts only for work the company has authorised.
- Keep credentials private and never share a password or session token.
- Report suspected phishing, lost devices or unusual account activity to the
  security team the same day.
- Treat other people's work and personal data with the same care as your own.

## Prohibited behaviour

1. Installing unapproved software on a managed device.
2. Copying customer data to personal storage or personal email.
3. Using company systems to harass, discriminate or break the law.
4. Attempting to bypass access controls or monitoring.

## Monitoring and review

WorkBot logs access to production systems so that incidents can be
investigated. Monitoring is limited to what is needed for security and
compliance, and is reviewed by the operations team every quarter.

Questions about this policy go to the operations team. Related guidance is in
the [Information Security Policy](/policies/information-security).

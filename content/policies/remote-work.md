## Where you can work

Most roles can work remotely. Work from a place with a reliable connection,
a private space for calls and enough room to handle confidential information
safely. Do not work on customer data in public spaces where the screen can be
read by others.

## Core hours

Everyone overlaps with their team from 10:00 to 15:00 in the team's local
time. Outside those hours, arrange your day around your responsibilities and
the needs of the people you work with.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office allowance. Equipment
remains company property and must be returned when you leave. Report faults
to the operations team rather than repairing them yourself.

## Working across time zones

- Share your working hours in your calendar and profile.
- Record meetings that others cannot attend.
- Agree on a response expectation with your team for urgent messages.

Managers review remote arrangements with their team at least twice a year.

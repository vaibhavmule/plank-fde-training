## Access

The admin console lives at `/admin` and is available to team leads and administrators. It has no separate sign-in of its own yet; access follows the account role.

## The overview

The overview shows one card per status with a count and the five most recent requests in that status. Counts refresh on every visit, so a page left open does not show live numbers.

## Settings

The settings page holds the deployment environment, the commit running in production and the health check link. Changing a setting here applies to every user, so the page is limited to administrators and records who changed what.

## Team and role changes

Adding a person to a team, or promoting someone to lead, is done from the people section of the knowledge base process rather than the console: file a request with the operations team. The console shows the result once the change is made.

## What the console does not do

The console does not edit knowledge documents, send customer messages or approve spending. Each of those belongs to the workflow that owns it, which keeps the audit trail in one place.

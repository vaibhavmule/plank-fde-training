## What a dashboard is

A dashboard is a page of cards that summarise live data. You describe what you want in a sentence and the builder proposes cards; you confirm before anything is saved.

## What it can show

- Counts of requests by team, status or priority.
- A trend over the last 7, 30 or 90 days.
- A table of the most recent or oldest rows in a filter.

## Writing the request

Name the measure, the grouping and the period: "Requests by team, this month, with a count and a trend". A dashboard that asks for two periods is two dashboards.

## Limits

A dashboard has at most eight cards. Each card reads the same stored data the overview does, so a card never disagrees with the list behind it.

## Saving and sharing

Save a dashboard to keep it, and share the link with your team. A shared dashboard reads live data, so a teammate sees their own permissions applied.

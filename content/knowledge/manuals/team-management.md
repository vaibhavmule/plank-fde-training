## The four roles

- **Member:** files requests, comments, reads the knowledge base.
- **Lead:** everything a member does, plus approving within their limits and managing their team's queue.
- **Administrator:** everything a lead does, plus the admin console and settings.
- **Auditor:** reads everything, changes nothing, including the request history.

## Changing a role

File a request with the people team naming the person, the role and the reason. The change is made within two business days and appears in the admin console.

## Removing a role

Roles are removed the same way, and access is removed within one business day when someone changes role or leaves, as the information security policy requires.

## Delegates

A lead going away for more than three days names a delegate in settings. A delegate acts with the lead's limits for the period and the history records the delegation.

## Who can be a lead

A lead must be a member of the team they lead. One person may lead two teams; the approval queue then shows both, separated by team.

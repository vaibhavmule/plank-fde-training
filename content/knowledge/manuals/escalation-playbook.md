## Triage in the first 15 minutes

1. Confirm the customer and the account.
2. Reproduce the problem, or record why you cannot.
3. Choose a severity from the escalation policy table.
4. Open a request with the customer, impact and steps tried.

## Severity and first response

A critical issue, such as a full outage or exposed data, needs a first response in 15 minutes and engineering on-call. A high issue, where a core workflow is broken for a team, needs one hour. Normal issues need one business day.

## What to tell the customer

Share the current status and the next update time. Never promise a fix date, and never say an issue is fixed until the owner confirms it in the request.

## Handing to engineering

Tag engineering on-call in the support channel with the request link. Engineering owns the request until it closes; support stays the customer's single point of contact.

## Closing

Close when the customer confirms the workflow works, or when the owner records a workaround the customer accepted. Every critical escalation is reviewed in the next support retrospective.

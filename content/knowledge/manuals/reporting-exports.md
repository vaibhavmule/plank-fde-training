## What can be exported

The requests list and the admin overview both export a CSV of the rows currently filtered. Columns are fixed and include id, title, team, priority, status and created time.

## How to export

Apply the filter you want, then choose Export. The file is built in the background and appears in your downloads list when it is ready. A year of requests takes under a minute.

## What the export leaves out

Details, comments and attachments are not in the list export, because they can hold personal data. A full export needs an operations request with the reason recorded.

## Retention

Analytics exports are kept 13 months. The file itself is a snapshot and does not update after it is built.

## Numbers that disagree

The overview counts by status at the moment you open it. An export taken an hour earlier will not match. Use one or the other for a report, and say which one in the report.

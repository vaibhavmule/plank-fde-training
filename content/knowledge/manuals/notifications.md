## Events that notify

- A request is assigned to you or your team.
- A request waiting on you changes state.
- An approval is requested from you.
- A high-priority request is filed with your team.

## Where notifications go

In-product notifications appear in the bell menu. Email is sent for approvals and for high-priority requests only, so the inbox stays usable.

## Quiet hours

Set quiet hours in your profile. Notifications during quiet hours appear in the product when you next open it, and only an incident marked critical sends email.

## Reminders

An approval with no answer is reminded once after five business days. A request in progress with no comment for five business days is reminded once to its owner only.

## Turning a type off

Each event type can be turned off for yourself in the profile page. You cannot turn off approval requests, because the workflow would stall without them.

## If a notification never arrives

Check the profile first, then file an operations request. Include the request link and the time you expected the notification.

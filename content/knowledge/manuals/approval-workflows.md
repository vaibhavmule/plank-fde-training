## Requesting approval

Open a request, choose the approver's team, and name the decision you need in the title. Attach the numbers or the document the approver needs, because an approval without a figure will be returned.

## Who can approve

- **Team lead** for spending up to 500 and for time off.
- **Finance** for spending from 500 to 10,000.
- **Finance and leadership** above 10,000.
- **Support lead** for refunds above $200.

An approver may not approve their own request. If the only qualified approver is the requester, the next role up approves.

## Delegation

An approver going away for more than three days names a delegate in the settings page. The delegate sees the same queue and the history records that they acted as a delegate.

## When nobody approves

A request waiting for approval for more than five business days is sent once as a reminder. After ten business days the team lead is notified, and after fifteen the request is returned to the requester with a note, so it never sits invisible.

## Withdrawing

The requester may withdraw a request before it is approved. A withdrawn request closes with the reason recorded. Re-submitting needs a new request.

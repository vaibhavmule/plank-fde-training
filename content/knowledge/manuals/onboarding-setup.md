## Before you start

Confirm the team has a lead who can approve spending and time off. A team without a lead cannot use approval workflows, because there is nobody to approve.

## Create the team

1. Choose a short lowercase name that matches how people already talk about the team.
2. Name the lead and at least one delegate.
3. Add the members.
4. Set the default filters each member sees in their queue.

## Connect the queue

Points the team queue at the request types it owns. A support team owns customer escalations; a finance team owns purchases and expenses. A team that owns nothing sees an empty queue, which usually means this step was skipped.

## Name the documents

Every knowledge document has an owner in its front matter. Set the new team's documents to its lead, so a stale section has a name attached to it.

## First week

File one real request and take it through approval to closed. Teams that do this in week one find the gaps in their setup; teams that wait find them on their first busy day.

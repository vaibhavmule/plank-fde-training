## Before you buy

Confirm that the need is real, that no existing tool covers it, and that the
spending fits your team's budget. Security and legal review are required
before signing anything that handles customer data.

## Approval thresholds

- Up to 500: team lead approval.
- 500 to 10,000: finance approval.
- Above 10,000: finance and leadership approval.

Renewals count toward the threshold. Splitting a purchase to avoid approval
is not allowed.

## Choosing a supplier

1. Compare at least two options for purchases above 5,000.
2. Check the supplier's security posture and data processing terms.
3. Confirm exit terms, notice periods and data export options.
4. Record the decision and its reasoning in the request.

## After purchase

Finance records the renewal date and owner. The owner reviews usage before
renewal and cancels tools that are no longer needed. Keep contracts and
invoices linked to the original request.

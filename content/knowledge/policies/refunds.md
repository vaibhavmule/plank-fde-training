## Refund authority

A support agent may refund up to $200 per customer without approval. The charge
must be no more than 90 days old.

## Larger refunds

A refund above $200 needs written approval from the support lead. Refunds above
$1,000 also need approval from finance.

## What to record

Record the customer, the charge, the amount and the reason in the request. Do
not promise a refund before it has been approved.

## Exceptions

A duplicate charge or a confirmed outage may be refunded above the standard
limit at the support lead's discretion.

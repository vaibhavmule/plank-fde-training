## When to escalate

Escalate a customer issue when any of the following is true:

- The customer cannot sign in or use a core workflow.
- Data is missing, incorrect or exposed.
- The issue is affecting more than one customer.
- A commitment made to the customer is at risk.

## Severity levels

| Severity | Example                         | First response | Escalate to         |
| -------- | ------------------------------- | -------------- | ------------------- |
| Critical | Full outage or data exposure    | 15 minutes     | Engineering on-call |
| High     | Core workflow broken for a team | 1 hour         | Support lead        |
| Normal   | Single-customer bug or question | 1 business day | Support lead        |

## How to escalate

1. Open a request with the customer, impact and steps already tried.
2. Set the priority from the table above.
3. Post the request link in the support channel and tag the next owner.
4. Stay with the customer until the request is acknowledged.

Do not promise a fix date. Share the current status and the next update time
instead. Review every critical escalation in the following week's support
retrospective.

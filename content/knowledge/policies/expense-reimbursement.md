## What you can claim

Reasonable costs that are directly connected to your work are reimbursable,
including:

- Travel and accommodation approved before booking.
- Meals while travelling for work.
- Software and equipment approved by your manager.
- Registration for work-related conferences.

## Limits

| Item                    | Limit                     |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Hotel per night         | 180 in the local currency |
| Dinner while travelling | 45 in the local currency  |
| Home internet           | 40 per month              |

Amounts above a limit need written approval from your manager before the
expense is committed.

## How to claim

1. Add the expense within 30 days of the purchase.
2. Attach an itemised receipt. Photos are fine.
3. Choose the correct team and cost centre.
4. Submit for manager approval.

Expenses submitted more than 60 days after the purchase need a written
explanation. The finance team replies to every submission within five
business days.

## Not covered

Personal upgrades, fines, alcohol beyond a team event, and anything already
paid for on a company card.

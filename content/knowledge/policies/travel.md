## Booking travel

Book through the travel desk so fares stay on the company account and the trip
can be changed when plans move. Do not book separately and claim the cost back
unless the desk has no route for it, which happens rarely. Get approval from
your manager before you book anything that leaves your region.

## What the company pays for

- Economy travel for flights under six hours, and premium economy with a
  manager's approval for longer ones.
- Hotels within the nightly limit for the city in the travel desk guide.
- Local transport between the airport, the hotel and the work site.
- Meals while travelling, up to the daily limit.

Anything else needs approval before you spend it. [Expense reimbursement](/policies/expense-reimbursement)
covers receipts and the submission window.

## Working while travelling

Travel time that falls in your normal working hours counts as work. Do not
schedule meetings you cannot attend from the airport or the train, and tell
your team when you are on the move and slow to reply.

## Safety and insurance

- Check the travel advisory for the destination before you book.
- Keep the company's emergency contact in your phone.
- Report an injury or a stolen device to the operations team the same day.
- [Remote work](/policies/remote-work) rules apply to the day you arrive and
  the day you leave.

## After the trip

Submit receipts within 14 days of returning. Add a short note on what the trip
achieved so the operations team can keep the travel budget accountable.

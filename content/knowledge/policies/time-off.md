## Holiday

Everyone receives 25 days of paid holiday each year, plus public holidays in
their country. Holiday accrues monthly and carries over up to 5 days into the
next year.

## Requesting time off

1. Check the team calendar and your open requests.
2. Submit the request at least two weeks before the first day, or four weeks
   for leave longer than a week.
3. Wait for your manager to approve it before booking travel.
4. Hand over urgent work and set an out-of-office reply.

## Sick leave

Tell your manager as soon as you are unable to work. You do not need to share
medical details. A doctor's note is required only for absences longer than
five working days.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave is available after one year of service. Discuss the request with
your manager and the people team at least six weeks in advance.

## Returning to work

On your first day back, check your inbox and requests for anything that
changed while you were away. Your manager will help you re-plan the week if
priorities moved.

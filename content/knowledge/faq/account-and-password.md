## Try these first

1. Use the password reset link, which goes to the account's admin email.
2. Check the spam folder for the reset email.
3. Confirm the email address has no typo, and that it is the one the account was created with.
4. Try a private browser window, in case a stale session is the cause.

## Single sign-on accounts

If the account uses single sign-on, the password lives with the customer's identity provider, not with WorkBot. Their IT team resets it. Support cannot reset an SSO password.

## Locked out after too many attempts

An account locks for 15 minutes after 10 failed attempts. Waiting is the fix; support cannot shorten the lock.

## A team member left

An account administrator removes the person and reassigns their work. Support does not remove users from an account, because only the customer knows who should still have access.

## When to contact support

Contact support when the reset email never arrives after 30 minutes, when the account is locked repeatedly, or when a former colleague still appears as an administrator. These are account-security issues and are treated as high priority.

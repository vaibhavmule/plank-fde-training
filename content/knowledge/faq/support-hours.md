## Support hours

Support answers from 08:00 to 18:00 in the customer's local time zone on business days. The queue is not staffed at weekends except for critical incidents.

## Severity and response

A critical issue, with a full outage or exposed data, is answered in 15 minutes at any hour through the on-call rota. A high issue, where a core workflow is broken, is answered within one hour during support hours. A normal question is answered within one business day.

## Outside support hours

A normal or high issue filed outside hours waits for the next business day. A critical issue pages the on-call engineer automatically, which is why marking a non-critical issue as critical delays real incidents.

## Holidays

Support follows public holidays in the customer's region, and a notice in the product names the dates. The on-call rota runs through holidays for critical issues.

## Escalation

If an issue is not moving, the customer can ask the support lead to take it over from the account's support thread. Every critical escalation is reviewed the following week.

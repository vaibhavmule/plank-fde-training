## Finding invoices

Invoices are in the billing section of the account, newest first, as PDFs. An administrator on the account can download any invoice; other users see only the ones they were copied on.

## What an invoice shows

The billing period, the plan, the seats and the tax charged. A tax identifier on the account appears on the invoice from the next billing period, not on invoices already issued.

## Changing billing details

An account administrator updates the company name, address and tax identifier in billing settings. Changes apply from the next invoice; an issued invoice is never edited, and a correction is a credit note instead.

## Changing the payment method

Cards are updated in billing settings. A bank transfer arrangement needs finance to set it up, which takes up to five business days.

## A failed payment

A failed payment is retried three times over five days, with email to the billing contact each time. Service continues during that window. If all three fail, the account moves to a limited state until a payment succeeds.

## Receipts

A receipt is sent by email to the billing contact on every successful payment. The invoice is the document to use for accounting, not the receipt.

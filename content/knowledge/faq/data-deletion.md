## What can be deleted

An account administrator can delete individual requests, contacts and attachments. Deleting removes them from the account and from search within a few minutes.

## What is kept after deletion

Backups keep a copy for 35 days, as the data retention policy states, and are never edited in place. Application logs keep the record of the deletion for 90 days. After those windows nothing remains.

## Closing the whole account

Closing an account is a support request that an administrator must confirm in writing. The account is closed at the end of the billing period, and the data is deleted on the schedule above.

## Legal holds

If a legal hold applies, deletion is paused for the affected records and the customer is told. This is rare and is recorded with the reason.

## What deletion is not

Deleting a record does not undo a refund or an invoice. Accounting records follow the retention rules for financial data, so support explains that difference before an account is closed.

## Requesting deletion

File the request from an administrator account, name the records, and confirm whether the account stays open. Support completes it within 5 business days and confirms when it is done.

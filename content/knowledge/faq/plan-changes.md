---
title: "Can I change my plan mid-month?"
owner: Finance team
updated: 2026-08-20
summary: "How upgrades, downgrades and seat changes are billed."
---

## Upgrades

An upgrade applies immediately. The customer is charged the difference for the rest of the current period, prorated by day, and the next period bills at the new rate.

## Downgrades

A downgrade applies at the start of the next period, so the customer keeps what they paid for until then. There is no refund for unused time on the higher plan.

## Adding seats

Seats added mid-period are prorated for the days remaining, in the same way as an upgrade. Seats removed take effect at renewal.

## Yearly plans

A yearly plan can move to a higher tier mid-term with the difference prorated. Moving down is done at renewal. Cancelling a yearly plan mid-term does not refund the remaining months.

## Trials

A trial converts to a paid plan on the date shown at sign-up unless it is cancelled before then. Support can confirm the conversion date; finance makes any exception.

## Where to change a plan

An account administrator changes the plan in billing settings. Changes that need a contract signature go through the account's sales contact instead.

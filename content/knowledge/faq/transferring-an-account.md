title: "How do I transfer my account to a colleague?"
owner: Support lead
updated: 2026-09-06
summary: "How account ownership moves when someone changes role or leaves."
---

## Who can start the transfer

An account administrator starts the transfer. If the person leaving is the only administrator, support adds the incoming administrator first, after verifying the request through the account's billing contact.

## What transfers

Ownership of the account passes, and the new administrator takes over billing, integrations and API keys. Requests and contacts stay with the account throughout, so nothing is copied or lost.

## What does not transfer

Personal notifications, saved filters and any draft content stay with the person who leaves. A draft that must survive is shared with the team before the account changes hands.

## API keys

Keys created by the person leaving are revoked during the transfer. The new administrator issues replacements, which is the safest point to rotate them anyway.

## Timing

Support completes a transfer within 2 business days of a verified request. Do a transfer before someone's last day, not after, so the account never sits without an active administrator.

## After the transfer

The new owner confirms billing details and reconnects any integration that used the old owner's authorisation. Support checks that at least one other administrator exists, so the account is never left with one owner.

## Export it yourself

An account administrator can export requests, contacts and the audit log from the account settings as CSV or JSON. The export covers the whole account, not one user.

## What needs a support request

Attachments and message history in bulk are exported by support after a short verification. Support confirms the requester is an administrator, then prepares the export.

## How long it takes

A self-service export is usually ready in minutes. A support-prepared export is delivered within 5 business days, through a time-limited download link rather than email.

## Format

JSON keeps the structure, including linked records. CSV is one row per record and flattens links to identifiers. For a migration, take the JSON.

## After export

The download link expires after 7 days. Support does not keep a copy, so download it before it expires. Deleting the account after an export follows the retention rules: some records are kept for a period after closure, which support will explain.

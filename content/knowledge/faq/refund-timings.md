## Once a refund is approved

A support agent may approve up to $200 per customer, and a refund above $200 needs the support lead's written approval. Approval is the point the clock starts.

## The timeline

- **Card payments:** 5 to 10 business days after approval, depending on the customer's bank.
- **Bank transfers:** 3 to 5 business days.
- **Invoices already paid:** a credit note is issued within 2 business days and the money follows the payment terms.

## What the customer sees

The customer receives a confirmation as soon as the refund is approved. The amount appears on their statement under the original merchant name, which sometimes confuses customers who expect the WorkBot name.

## When it is late

If the money has not arrived 10 business days after approval, support asks for a bank reference and re-checks the refund. A refund that shows as completed on our side but is missing at the bank is escalated to finance.

## The 90-day rule

A charge older than 90 days cannot be refunded through the standard flow, because the card networks limit it. Those cases go to the support lead as an exception.

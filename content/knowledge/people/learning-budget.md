## The budget

Every person has 1,500 a year for learning, plus two conference days. The budget resets in January and does not carry over, so plan it through the year rather than in December.

## What it covers

- Courses and books directly connected to your work.
- Conference tickets, including a workshop day.
- Certification exams, and one retake.
- A subscription tool you need to study, if IT does not already provide it.

## What it does not cover

Travel to a conference is claimed from the travel budget, not this one, so a trip needs both approvals. Equipment is bought by IT rather than from the learning budget.

## Claiming

Raise the expense with the receipt and a line on what you learned. Amounts above 500 need your manager's approval before you book, in line with the expense reimbursement policy.

## Time away

Conference days are working days, so no holiday is used. A multi-day conference that exceeds two days uses holiday for the extra days unless your manager agrees otherwise.

## Sharing back

After a conference, add a short note to your team's channel on the one thing worth adopting. Sharing what you learned is how the budget pays back more than once.

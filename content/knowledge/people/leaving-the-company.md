## Notice

The notice period is one month for most roles and three months for leads. Either side can give notice; the people team confirms the last working day in writing.

## Handover

Before your last week, write a handover note: open work, who owns what next, and where the files live. Your manager reviews it with you so nothing depends on your memory.

## Access

Access is removed on your last working day, which the information security policy requires within one business day. Equipment is returned through an operations request, arranged before you leave.

## Final pay

Final pay covers the days worked, unused holiday that the policy allows, and any approved expenses. It arrives on the next normal pay date.

## Knowledge you own

Every knowledge document has an owner. Hand your documents to a named person and ask the operations team to change the owner, so no section loses its owner when you go.

## Exit conversation

The people team offers a 30-minute exit conversation. It is optional, and what you say is kept confidential unless you agree otherwise.

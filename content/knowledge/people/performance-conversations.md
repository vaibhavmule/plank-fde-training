## The rhythm

A manager and each report meet every two weeks for a conversation about work in progress. Once a month the conversation sets aside 30 minutes for performance: what is working, what is not.

## What a good conversation contains

- One thing that went well, with the evidence.
- One thing to change, stated as a behaviour rather than a personality.
- The next step that follows from each.

## When something needs to change quickly

If a problem is serious, the conversation happens immediately rather than at the next meeting. The manager writes down what was agreed and shares it with the report and the people team.

## Raising a concern upward

A report who is not getting useful feedback says so to the manager first. If it persists, the people team is the next step; it keeps the concern confidential.

## Recording

Managers record a short note after the monthly conversation. The note is visible to the report. It is not a score, and it is not used as a secret file.

## Between reviews

Regular conversations exist so the review holds no surprises. A review that contains feedback you have never heard is a fault of the conversations, not of the review.

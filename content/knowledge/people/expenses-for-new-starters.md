## The home office allowance

Every new starter receives a one-time home office allowance. Raise it as an expense in the first month; the finance team approves it without a cost centre, because a new starter may not have one yet.

## Travel before your start date

Travel to a first-day induction is booked by the people team, so do not claim it yourself. Travel in your first week for a work reason is claimed as normal.

## Software you need on day one

Ask IT rather than buying. A tool from the approved catalogue installs without a cost. Only software outside the catalogue is bought, and that goes through procurement.

## Receipts

Keep the itemised receipt, not just the card slip, and submit within 30 days. The expense reimbursement policy sets the limits; nothing changes for a new starter except the cost centre.

## If a claim is rejected

A rejection names the reason. Correct it and resubmit within the 60-day window. A claim that is approaching 60 days and is stuck on a cost centre goes to the finance team with a note.

## Telling your manager

Tell your manager as soon as you know you cannot work, by message rather than by silence. You do not need to share medical details, and a manager should not ask for them.

## How much is paid

Sick leave is paid in full for up to 20 days in any 12 months. Beyond that, the people team talks with you about a longer arrangement.

## A doctor's note

A note is needed only for an absence longer than five working days. The time off policy says the same; send it to the people team, not to your manager, so it stays in one place.

## During the absence

Set an out-of-office message and name someone who can answer urgent questions. You are not expected to read messages, and nothing that arrives while you are away should wait only for you.

## Returning

On your first day back, check what moved while you were away and agree with your manager on what to drop. A phased return is available after a long absence.

## Repeated short absence

Repeated short absences are handled as a wellbeing conversation, not a disciplinary one, unless a pattern of avoidable absence is clear.

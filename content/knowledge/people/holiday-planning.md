## The allowance

Everyone receives 25 days of paid holiday a year plus public holidays, and up to 5 days carry into the next year. Holiday accrues monthly, so a long break in January is easier after a full year.

## How much notice

Submit at least two weeks before the first day, and four weeks for a break longer than a week. The time off policy states the same, and a late request may be declined even when the allowance is available.

## Cover

Name a cover person for anything urgent, and hand over open requests. A request with no owner while you are away is the most common cause of a missed deadline.

## Team limits

A team keeps at least half its people working on any business day, and at least one lead or delegate. School holiday weeks fill first, so book them early.

## Personal days

For a day you need at short notice, a personal day needs no notice beyond telling your manager. Personal days come out of the holiday allowance, so they are not extra.

## Unused days

Days that cannot carry over are used by the end of the year or lost. Managers are reminded in November to help their team plan them.

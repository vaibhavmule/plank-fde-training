## The levels

The engineering, product and support tracks each have four levels: associate, mid, senior and lead. A level describes the scope of your work, not how long you have been here.

## The review cycle

Reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each cycle looks at the work from the previous two quarters.

## What the review looks at

1. **Outcomes:** what changed because of your work.
2. **Scope:** how much you owned end to end.
3. **Collaboration:** how you helped others do their best work.
4. **Craft:** the quality and reliability of what you built.

## Preparing

Before the review, write one page: the three outcomes you are proudest of, with the evidence, and the one thing you want to grow next. Your manager does the same for you.

## Promotion

A promotion needs evidence at the next level already, sustained for at least one cycle. A promotion conversation is separate from the review score, so a strong score does not by itself imply a promotion.

## The conversation

Your manager shares feedback, discusses your growth plan, and records a summary in the people system. You can add your own note to the record.

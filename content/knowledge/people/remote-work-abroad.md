## Short trips

You may work from another country for up to 30 days in a rolling 12 months without a review. Tell your manager and record the dates in your calendar and the team calendar.

## Longer stays

A stay beyond 30 days needs approval from your manager and the people team before you book. Approval looks at time zone overlap, the security of the place you will work from, and tax.

## Tax and payroll

Working from a country for more than 183 days in a year can create a tax obligation for the company in that country. The people team reviews this with finance before any long stay, and the answer can be no.

## Security

Remote work rules apply abroad: a private space, a locked screen and no customer data visible in public. Some countries restrict encrypted tools, so check with security before a long stay.

## What is not allowed

Do not work from a country under a company travel advisory, and do not take company equipment somewhere it cannot be insured. A permanent move abroad is a new employment contract, which the people team handles separately.

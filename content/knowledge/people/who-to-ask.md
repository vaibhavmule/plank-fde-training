## Money

Ask **finance** about invoices, expenses, procurement, licences, pay and billing. A spending approval up to 500 comes from your team lead; from 500 to 10,000 from finance.

## People

Ask the **people team** about time off beyond the standard request, roles, team changes, onboarding and leaving. Your manager approves standard holiday.

## Systems and devices

Ask **IT** about laptops, sign-in, the VPN, software installs, printers and drives. A lost device is also a security incident, so tell **security** the same day.

## Customers

Ask the **support lead** about a customer escalation, a refund above the limit, or a customer asking for something outside the standard flow.

## Security

Ask the **security team** about a suspected phishing message, a possible breach, a shared credential or an access review. Report first, investigate later.

## Policy questions

Ask **operations** when the knowledge base does not answer a policy question. A gap in the corpus is itself a request: file it so a document gets written.

## How to ask

File a request rather than a direct message. A request has an owner, a status and a history, and it survives someone being on leave.

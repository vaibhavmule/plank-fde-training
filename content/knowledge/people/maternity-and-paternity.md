## The amount

Every parent is entitled to 26 weeks of paid parental leave for a birth, adoption or surrogacy, regardless of gender or of how long they have been with the company.

## Keeping in touch

You may work up to 10 keeping-in-touch days during leave, paid at your normal rate. These are optional and cannot be required by a manager.

## Requesting leave

Tell your manager and the people team as early as is practical, and at least 8 weeks before the leave is due to start. The people team confirms the dates and the pay in writing.

## Changing the dates

The leave may start up to 8 weeks before the expected date and be taken in one block or split into two. A change needs two weeks' notice and a message to the people team.

## Someone covering the work

Your manager names a cover person before the leave starts, and you hand over open work to that person. Reviews due during leave move to the cycle after your return.

## Returning

You may return part time for the first three months, with your manager's agreement and no change to your level. The people team adjusts the pay and the record.

## Printing

Printers are on each floor and appear automatically when you are on the office network. Release a job with your badge or your work account at the printer.

## Customer data on paper

Print customer data only when there is no digital path. The printed copy counts as a copy of the record, so it must be destroyed when it is no longer needed. Use the shredding bin, never the general waste.

## Scanning

The scanner sends a PDF to your work address. For a document that must reach a customer, upload it in the request rather than emailing it from the scanner.

## Confidential documents

A document marked confidential is printed to a named printer and collected immediately. Do not leave it in the tray; the printer clears uncollected jobs after two hours.

## When a printer will not work

Check the display for a paper or toner message first, then try the printer on another floor. If it still fails, file an operations request with the floor and the printer name.

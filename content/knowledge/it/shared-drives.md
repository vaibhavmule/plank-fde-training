## Where files belong

Team files go in the team's shared drive. Personal work in progress goes in your own folder inside it. Files that must be found later go in the drive, not in a chat conversation.

## Who can see a drive

A drive inherits its members from the team. A guest outside the team needs a share with an explicit expiry, and a folder that holds customer data is never shared outside the company.

## Naming

Use a date prefix and a short description: `2026-11 travel-policy-review`. A folder named after a person becomes useless when that person moves, so name folders after the work.

## When someone leaves

Files transfer to the team lead. The drive is not deleted. Shared links that pointed at the person's personal folder stop working, which is intended.

## Retention

Files follow the data retention policy: customer request material is kept 3 years after the request closes, and application logs 90 days. Do not keep a personal archive of exports that the policy would delete.

## Degraded performance

A sync client that stops is restarted from the menu bar. If a file conflicts, keep both copies and ask the team lead which one is authoritative.

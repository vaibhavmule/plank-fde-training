## Your mailbox

The mailbox is a work account with a 50 GB limit. Archive rather than delete when you approach it; archived mail is searchable and does not count toward the limit.

## Shared calendars

Each team has a shared calendar showing leave and travel. Put your leave on your own calendar plus the team calendar, so the queue can be planned around it.

## Meeting rooms

Rooms are booked from the calendar invite. A room with no booking can be used but must be released if a meeting is booked for it. Cancel the room when the meeting moves online.

## Sending customer data

Send customer data only to a company address, and only what the recipient needs. Never send it to a personal address, and never forward a whole thread when one message answers the question.

## Suspicious email

Do not click, do not reply, and do not open the attachment. Report it with the report button, which sends the message to security. A message that appears to come from a colleague asking for a payment is reported, never answered.

## Leaving the company

The mailbox is disabled the same day and kept for 30 days, then deleted. Set an out-of-office before your last day so senders know who to contact.

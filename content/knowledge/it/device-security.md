## What every managed device has

Disk encryption, automatic operating system updates, endpoint protection and a screen lock that engages within five minutes. These are enforced by policy and cannot be turned off.

## Reporting a lost device

Report it immediately, in the same hour if you can. The security team can revoke the device's sessions and lock it remotely. A device that is offline is locked the next time it connects.

1. File an operations request marked high.
2. Say when it was last seen and whether it was locked.
3. Change your primary password from another device.
4. Do not try to locate the device yourself if doing so risks your safety.

## A stolen device

Treat a stolen device as a security incident. The security team decides whether to involve the police, and records the decision in the request.

## Returning equipment

Equipment is returned when you leave or change role. The operations team arranges collection; do not ship a device yourself unless the request says to.

## Personal devices

Personal devices may read email and the knowledge base through the browser. They may not hold customer data, and they get no access to internal admin tools.

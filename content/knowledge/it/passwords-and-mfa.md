## Storing passwords

Use the approved password manager for every work credential. Never store a work password in a browser, a spreadsheet or a note app. Never reuse a work password on a personal site.

## What makes a strong password

Length matters more than symbols. Use at least 16 characters, or let the password manager generate one. Avoid the company name, the product name and anything on your public profile.

## Passkeys

Where the application supports a passkey, use one. A passkey is bound to the device, so it cannot be phished the way a typed password can.

## MFA methods

The authenticator app is the default. Hardware keys are issued to administrators and to anyone who asks. SMS is a fallback only, because a swapped SIM defeats it.

## Resetting MFA

A lost or replaced device needs an operations request marked high. The security team verifies your identity by video call and issues a new enrolment within one business day.

## Sharing

Never share a password, a session token or an MFA code, including with a colleague covering for you. Use delegation instead, which the team management guide covers.

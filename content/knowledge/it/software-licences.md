## Buying a licence

Buy through procurement. A licence is a purchase like any other, so the approval thresholds apply: up to 500 needs a team lead, from 500 to 10,000 needs finance, and above 10,000 needs finance and leadership.

## Seats

Buy the number of seats you can justify this quarter, not the number you hope to use. Adding seats later is quick; unused seats are money that does not come back.

## Who owns a renewal

The person who filed the purchase owns the renewal. Finance records the renewal date against that person. An owner who leaves hands the licence to the team lead, who becomes the owner.

## Before a renewal

Review usage in the two weeks before the renewal date. Cancel a tool that is no longer used; the procurement policy expects this review, and finance asks for it on any renewal above 5,000.

## Dormant licences

A licence with no sign-in for 90 days is reported to the owner. If nobody claims it, finance cancels it at the next renewal.

## Sharing a licence

Sharing a named-user licence breaks the supplier's terms and is not allowed. If the cost is the reason, ask finance about a shared plan instead.

## Before you open it

Collect the laptop from IT or confirm the courier delivery. Do not buy a laptop yourself and claim it back; equipment comes from IT so it can be enrolled and tracked.

## First sign-in

1. Connect to power and the network.
2. Sign in with your work account; the first sign-in enrols the device.
3. Wait for the setup profile to finish; it installs disk encryption, updates and endpoint protection.
4. Restart once, as prompted, then sign in again.

## What you may install

Software from the approved catalogue installs without asking. Anything else needs an operations request with the name and the reason; unapproved software on a managed device is prohibited by the acceptable use policy.

## What IT controls

Disk encryption, automatic updates, endpoint protection and remote wipe stay under IT's control. These settings cannot be turned off by the user, and attempting to is a security incident.

## Home office allowance

The company pays a one-time home office allowance. Raise the expense through the reimbursement policy; equipment bought with it remains company property.
